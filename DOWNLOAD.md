- Task package: https://github.com/large-road-damage-datalake/uapd-object-detection

## Notes

- This package uses local UAPD VOC annotations under a single dataset split.
- Source paper reports 3,151 images and six distress categories.
- Local annotations include rare Block crack labels in addition to the six primary classes.
